# Kpproof

Mobile friendly webapp generate and Date auto selected today, Party Name as per Party Master active Party Name showing, Vehcile No, Ltr, Amount, Attach Capture Image

A complete mobile-friendly web application for fuel and transport fleet logging (FuelLog Pro) tailored to your exact specifications:

Key Screens Created:
New Fuel Entry (New Fuel Entry):

Auto-Selected Today's Date: Automatically presets today's date (Today, Oct 24, 2024 with current timestamp).
Party Name (Party Master Linked): Highlights active party selection (e.g., Apex Logistics Pvt Ltd with an Active status chip and easy master switch).
Vehicle No Input: High-contrast input with quick-tap pills for recently refueled fleet vehicles.
Volume (Ltr) & Amount Calculation: Stepper input with quick addition increments (+25L, +50L, Full Tank) and automatic rate calculation (e.g. ₹94.50/L yielding ₹14,175.00).
Attach & Capture Image: Dedicated camera receipt/meter photo section with OCR verification indicator, retake actions, and preview slips.
Quick Actions: Prominent primary button to Save & Log Entry as well as Save & Print Thermal Slip.
Party Master (Party Master):

Comprehensive directory of enrolled parties with active/inactive indicators, vehicle counts, credit limits, and quick "Assign to Entry" actions.
Fuel History Logs (Fuel History Logs):

Shift telemetry card showing total liters dispensed, total monetary volume, and logged vouchers.
Chronological log entries displaying vehicle number, party name, volume, amount, and proof slip verification status.
Receipt & Meter Camera Capture (Receipt & Meter Capture):

Interactive camera viewfinder with AR bounding box for OCR reading of dispenser meters, slip photo upload, flash toggle, and one-tap reading verification.
